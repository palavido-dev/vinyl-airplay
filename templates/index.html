Mobile Access

To use the barcode scanner or camera features on your phone, your phone needs to trust this device's certificate. Install it once and you're set.

Download Certificate
Setup instructions
iPhone / iPad:
1. Tap "Download Certificate" above
2. Go to Settings > General > VPN and Device Management
3. Tap the Vinyl Streamer profile and install it
4. Go to Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings
5. Enable full trust for the Vinyl Streamer certificate

Android:
1. Tap "Download Certificate" above
2. Go to Settings > Security > Encryption and Credentials
3. Tap "Install a certificate" > "CA certificate"
4. Select the downloaded file

Then access the app at https://vinyl.local:8443
