What’s in the Studio build

A listening-room appliance, not a pile of spare cables. The reference hardware is chosen for clean capture, reliable storage, and a touchscreen that feels like furniture.

Compute & audio

Raspberry Pi 5 with a HiFiBerry DAC2 ADC Pro for line-level vinyl capture and local playback.

Storage & display

NVMe storage for your FLAC library, plus a 10.1" touchscreen running the jukebox UI in kiosk mode.

Your room

Works with a turntable that has line-level output (or a phono preamp), and AirPlay or Bluetooth speakers you already own.
