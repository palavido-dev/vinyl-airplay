Cheaper parts, same idea

The Studio BOM works. Next is proving what can get cheaper without making the listening experience worse.

Keep for now

Raspberry Pi ecosystem. Audio HATs, kiosk Chromium, BlueALSA, and AirPlay tooling are already battle-tested here. Jumping platforms early adds driver risk for little gain at this volume.

Worth testing

Pi 4 · smaller NVMe / quality SD for lighter libraries · class-compliant USB ADCs · 7" displays · headless phone-UI SKU · quieter power and cabling kits.

Maybe later

Custom enclosure, flashable image, Crowd Supply / Kickstarter batch once a handful of hand-built units have paid customers and a settled BOM.
